01 — Executive Summary

The Opportunity

Rwanda continues to invest significantly in infrastructure development — roads, public buildings, water systems, urban development, and climate resilience projects.

Yet monitoring the execution and impact of these investments remains largely dependent on periodic reporting, physical inspections, and manually generated evidence. This creates a visibility gap:

  • Are projects progressing according to plan?
  • Are delays detected early enough?
  • Are public investments delivering expected outcomes?
  • Can decision-makers monitor hundreds of projects across the country efficiently?

ProjectWatch Rwanda is an AI-powered geospatial intelligence platform that transforms satellite imagery, GIS data, and project information into actionable insights for infrastructure monitoring. The platform provides an independent digital evidence layer enabling governments, development partners, and infrastructure stakeholders to monitor progress, identify risks, and make data-driven decisions.

02 — Vision

A national intelligence layer

To become Rwanda’s leading infrastructure intelligence platform, enabling evidence-based management of national development projects.

03 — Mission

What we do

To combine satellite technology, artificial intelligence, and modern software engineering to provide continuous visibility into physical development activities across Rwanda.

04 — Problem Statement

Current Challenges

Infrastructure project monitoring faces several limitations in how progress is observed, verified, and acted upon.

Limited Visibility

Project owners often receive progress updates only through scheduled reports.

Example

Monthly contractor report: “Road construction is 60% complete.”
However, there is limited independent validation between reporting cycles.

Manual Verification

Verification often requires site visits, physical inspections, photo evidence collection, and manual reporting — an approach that is expensive and difficult to scale.

Delayed Risk Detection

Problems such as construction delays, contractor under-performance, project inactivity, and land-use conflicts may only become visible after significant time has passed.
